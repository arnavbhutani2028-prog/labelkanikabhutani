Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

What we collect

When you place an order or book a consultation we collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, PIN code and, if you choose to share it, your wedding date and measurements. When you use the AI Stylist we process the preferences you enter (occasion, budget, colours, style) to generate recommendations.

How we use it

We use your information only to process and deliver your order, confirm measurements, send order updates, respond to your enquiries and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Service providers

We share the minimum necessary information with trusted providers who help us run the store: our payment processor (Razorpay), order database (Supabase), email provider (Resend), courier partners, AI provider (Google Gemini, for stylist recommendations, without your contact details), and website analytics (Microsoft Clarity, which records anonymised usage).

Cookies and analytics

We use essential browser storage to remember your bag, and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Please contact us.
