Shipping Policy

Made to measure, shipped free across India

Production time

Every outfit is made to order. Production takes 3–5 weeks from the date your measurements are confirmed. If you have a wedding date, add it at checkout and we will confirm whether we can deliver in time before production begins.

Shipping within India

Shipping is free on all orders within India. Orders are dispatched through reputed courier partners and usually reach you within 3–7 working days of dispatch. You will receive tracking details by SMS or email once your order ships.

International shipping

We currently ship only within India. For international enquiries, please contact us.

Delays

Delays caused by courier partners, weather or public holidays are outside our control, but we will keep you informed and help resolve any issue with the courier.

Damaged or missing parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged or tampered with, please record an unboxing video and contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
